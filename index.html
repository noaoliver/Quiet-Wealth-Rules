A letter before we begin

"My father was a good mechanic. He died owing money."

I grew up in a working family. My father never had savings, and that stayed with me. At twenty-seven I started at a small firm in Charleston, filing papers. An old banker, Mr. Coleman, took me under his wing and taught me the rules rich families never write down.

For forty years I helped more than sixty families with their money. I lived through 1987, 2000 and 2008. I saw who stayed rich and who didn't, and it was almost never about how much they earned.

These are the rules. I wish my father had had them.

— Harold
